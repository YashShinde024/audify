## 1. Research Methodology & Data Integrity

This research investigates publicly observable search intent, YouTube video title patterns, Google search autocomplete suggestions, and competitor taxonomy (Audioalter, AudioMass, 123apps, TwistedWave).

### Standards & Constraints:
- **No fabricated search volumes:** Exact monthly volume numbers are not claimed as verified rankings. Search demand is identified through observable query modifiers, autocomplete prioritization, YouTube title structures, and public forum requests.
- **Evidence-based mapping:** Every keyword cluster corresponds to an authentic, functional browser-based DSP engine tool or technical guide in Audify.
- **Strictly no thin doorway pages:** All articles in `/blog/*` and tool pages contain genuine mathematical DSP diagrams, parameter explanations, and direct deep-links to the live studio.

## 3. On-Page Implementation Strategy

1. **Unique Titles & Compelling Meta Descriptions:**
   - Every route and preset article includes descriptive `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` tags incorporating high-intent query modifiers while avoiding repetitive keyword stuffing.
2. **Standardized Open Graph & Twitter Card Previews:**
   - Rich social card metadata configured across all routes with canonical URL specification.
3. **Structured Data (Schema.org):**
   - Homepage & Studio: `WebApplication` + `AudioObject` schemas.
   - Blog & Guides: `TechArticle` + `FAQPage` schemas with step-by-step DSP routing.
4. **Internal Deep-Linking Architecture:**
   - Every blog post contains direct contextual links to both the relevant preset guide and the interactive Studio instance pre-configured with that preset.
